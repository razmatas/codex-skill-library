# Logo Inspo Reference

Use Logosystem as the logo-reference source. It is a curated, public gallery; use it to understand visual principles and construction choices, never to reproduce an existing logo.

## Research

Start from the brand brief: audience, category, personality, desired level of distinctiveness, naming constraints, existing brand assets, and application needs. Search and combine the relevant public filters: logo type, industry, style, shape, colour, and mood.

Keep the shortlist focused—three to five references is usually sufficient. Where relevant, include one motion or app-icon example. Trace references to their credited designer or original source when it helps establish context.

## Turn references into a logo direction

For each retained reference, extract transferable decisions only:

- mark architecture: wordmark, symbol, combination mark, or monogram;
- typographic voice: case, width, weight, contrast, and customisation;
- shape language: geometric, organic, monoline, negative-space, or emblematic;
- optical choices: silhouette, counterforms, rhythm, balance, and small-size legibility; and
- intended application: avatar, favicon, app icon, motion, signage, or lock-up.

Recommend one clear logo-system direction and state the rationale, the visual ingredients to use, and the characteristics to avoid. Do not copy a brand's logo, modify an existing mark, or present reference artwork as new original work.

## Access boundary

Logosystem has no connected MCP tool in this environment. Research its public archive through web browsing and preserve attribution and source links for retained references.
